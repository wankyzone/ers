# Founder Milestone — First Employee Separation

**Date:** 13 September 2026
**Project:** ERS / Wanky Software
**Milestone:** First employee separation

## What happened

13 September 2026 marks the first employee separation in the history of Wanky Software / ERS.

Mariam, who had been engaged as a frontend developer, exited the ERS project after informing us that she was no longer interested in continuing with the project and requesting that project-related emails stop.

## Operational decision

Mariam is no longer considered an active ERS contributor. Any remaining frontend responsibilities are to be reassigned according to the current Sprint 6 execution plan.

GitHub remains the engineering source of truth.

## Founder lesson

This is more than a personnel event. It is an early company-building lesson: people may join, leave, change direction, or become unavailable. The company must be structured so that the mission, codebase, documentation, and operating system survive those changes.

The appropriate response is to respect the person's decision, close the relationship professionally, protect company access and intellectual property, preserve the engineering audit trail, and keep execution moving.

This day is now part of the permanent ERS/Wanky Software operating history.
